FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still have questions? Contact support during onboarding.

How does onboarding start?
Every plan starts with guided onboarding. We help connect your stack, align controls, and launch evidence collection for your selected frameworks.
Which compliance frameworks do you support?
SOC 2 (Type I and II), HIPAA, ISO 27001, ISO 27701, GDPR, PCI DSS, NIST CSF, and CCPA out of the box. Enterprise customers can also add custom frameworks.
How does pricing work as we add frameworks?
Starter includes 1 framework. Growth includes 3. Enterprise includes unlimited frameworks. You can add individual frameworks to Starter or Growth for a flat per-framework monthly fee.
Do you integrate with our existing tools?
Kanrei integrates with AWS, GCP, Azure, GitHub, GitLab, Okta, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Jira, Slack, and 40+ more. Enterprise plans include custom integrations.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees on Starter or Growth. Enterprise plans include white-glove onboarding with a dedicated customer success manager at no extra cost.
Can we switch plans later?
Yes, any time. Upgrades are prorated from the day you switch. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. No long-term contracts required.
