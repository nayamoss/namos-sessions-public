FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does onboarding start?

Every plan starts with guided onboarding. We help connect your stack, align controls, and launch evidence collection for your selected frameworks.

Which frameworks do you support?

Out of the box: SOC 2 (Type I & II), HIPAA, ISO 27001, and GDPR. Enterprise plans unlock PCI-DSS, NIST 800-53, and custom framework mappings.

How does pricing work as we add frameworks?

Starter includes one framework. Growth includes three. On Enterprise, frameworks are unlimited. Switching or adding frameworks mid-plan is free — you only upgrade when you exceed your plan's allotment.

Do you integrate with our existing tools?

Yes. We connect with AWS, GCP, Azure, GitHub, GitLab, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Okta, Jira, Slack, and 40+ more. Growth and Enterprise plans unlock unlimited integrations.

Is there a setup fee?

No setup fees on any plan. Starter and Growth customers onboard themselves in under an hour. Enterprise customers get a dedicated onboarding specialist included in the contract.

Can we switch plans?

Anytime. Upgrades are prorated instantly. Downgrades take effect at the next billing period. Your data, policies, and evidence history transfer automatically.
